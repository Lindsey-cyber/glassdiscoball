The four ideas

Filtration. Put a ball of radius r around each point. When two balls overlap, join their centres by an edge; when three balls share a common point, fill in the triangle (the Čech complex). As r grows the complex only gains simplices: a nested family of shapes.

Birth and death. At r = 0 every point is its own component. When two components touch, the younger one dies and merges into the older (the elder rule). A loop is born when an edge closes a cycle and dies when triangles fill it in.

Barcode. One bar per feature, from birth to death. Long bars are real structure; short bars are noise. β0 and β1 at any r count the bars crossing the vertical line.

Persistence diagram. The same bars drawn as points (birth, death). Everything sits above the diagonal; the further from it, the longer the feature lived. Points in the shaded corner are alive at the current r.
